Feeding on the go, without the fuss

Making a bottle away from home usually means scooping, counting and spilling with a baby in one arm. We made it one press.

Drops, never pours

The powder falls straight down into the water through a sealed valve, so nothing lands on the rim or the table.

Measured at home

Fill three chambers in your clean kitchen. Every feed is ready to go, with no counting scoops in the car.

Locks for the bag

A click ring blocks the button so nothing drops by accident. Turn it to open only when it is feeding time.

How it works

  1. 1

    Fill at home

    Put one measured feed in each of the three chambers, then pop the lid back on.

  2. 2

    Twist to click

    Turn the dial until it clicks. The next full chamber lines up over the drop hole.

  3. 3

    Unlock and press

    Turn the lock ring to open and press the button. The feed drops into the water.

  4. 4

    Shake and feed

    Lock it again, shake, swap the dock for the teat, and feed.

Questions parents ask

Will it fit my baby's bottle?

It comes with a wide-neck collar in 50, 54 or 58 mm. For standard or narrow bottles, add the standard-neck adapter in 38, 41 or 44 mm. Send us your bottle brand on WhatsApp and we'll tell you which size to pick.

Can the button go off in my bag?

Not while it's locked. Turn the lock ring to L and its posts sit under the button so it can't be pressed. Turn it to O when you're ready to feed.

How much formula does each chamber hold?

About 19–23 g, which makes roughly 130–150 ml with many formulas. Check your tin's scoop-to-water ratio and fill to match.

Does all the powder come out?

The chambers have steep sloped floors and the valve is cone-shaped so the powder slides off. Hold the button for a second or two and give the dock a light tap.

How do I clean it?

The dock comes apart into its main pieces for washing. Dry every part fully before refilling, because damp powder clumps.

Is the water mixed inside the dock?

No. The powder stays dry in the dock until you press the button, and it only meets the water inside your bottle.
